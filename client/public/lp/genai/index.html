FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a coding background?

No. The course starts from fundamentals with Python, and mentors guide you month by month.

How long is the course, and how are classes held?

4 months of live classes, 5 days a week, with one phase each month: foundations and LLMs, Generative AI and RAG, Agentic AI, then cloud deployment and the capstone.

Is this only prompt engineering?

No. Prompting is covered in Month 1. Most of the course is about building RAG applications and AI agents with LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI and AutoGen, and deploying them on AWS.

Do I have to pay for AWS or other software?

AWS offers free credits for new accounts that cover practice in the course. Amounts are set by AWS and can change; your mentor will show you how to set budget alerts so you don't overspend.

Is a job guaranteed?

No institute can honestly guarantee a job. Every student gets placement support: technical resume, GitHub and LinkedIn optimisation, mock technical interviews and job referrals. Offers depend on your performance and the hiring company.

Is this a new course?

Yes, it is newly launched. It is taught by the same mentors and supported by the same Delta 360 live-project pipeline and placement team as our other programs.

What is the fee, and is EMI available?

Your counselor will share the current fee, any scholarship you qualify for, and the payment options on your free call.
